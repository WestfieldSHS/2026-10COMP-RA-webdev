Learn how to dribble

Dribbling is one of the most essential skills in football because it gives players the creative freedom to break through tight defensive structures and create goal-scoring opportunities. While passing moves the ball across the pitch, masterclass dribbling allows a player to look a defender in the eye, manipulate their balance, and completely eliminate them from the play. Maintaining close control of the ball at high speeds requires incredible agility, a low center of gravity, and split-second spatial awareness. By mastering the art of dribbling, a player changes the entire dynamic of an attack, drawing multiple defenders out of position and creating massive openings for their team.

Steps for driblling

  • 1. Keep the ball close to your feet by taking small, frequent touches with every single step you take. This high volume of contact makes it incredibly difficult for a defender to time a tackle or steal possession.
  • 2. Use the outside laces of your boot to push the ball forward while running at speed. This natural foot positioning allows you to maintain your momentum and sprint smoothly without breaking your stride.
  • 3. Lower your center of gravity by slightly bending your knees and leaning your torso forward. This athletic posture grants you the balance needed to change direction instantly and explode away from a defender.
  • 4. Keep your head up as much as possible rather than staring straight down at the ball. Developing this field vision allows you to scan for incoming defensive tackles while tracking the open space around you
